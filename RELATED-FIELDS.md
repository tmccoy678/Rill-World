# Developmental electropsychology among related fields

## A recurring term

**Developmental electropsychology** is Rillworld's working name for a
speculative biomedical-engineering theory. The phrase joins a recurring history
of electricity and psychology while giving the relationship a developmental,
wave-native, and potentially non-neural center.

| Period | Usage | Center of inquiry |
|---|---|---|
| **1850** | John Bovee Dods published *The Philosophy of Electrical Psychology* in the context of mesmerism, cure, nervous force, mind–body relations, and metaphysical electricity ([Dods, 1850](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/ancrzdyv)). | Electrical psychology as a philosophy of mind, health, influence, and “electro-nervous” force. |
| **1920s**, documented historically in 2001 | Weimar electrical psychodiagnosis brought psychology together with electrical engineering. “Diagnoscopy” attempted personality profiling by electrical means while EEG emerged nearby as a recording method ([Borck, 2001](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0269889701000254)). | Electricity as a medium for diagnosing or revealing an existing human psyche. |
| **2023** | Harisoorya and D'Mello explicitly used *electropsychology* for EEG and QEEG measures associated with mental-health conditions ([2023](https://doi.org/10.47992/IJHSP.2581.6411.0098)). A related *electropsychological* usage appears in ERP research on emotional arousal ([Yusoff et al., 2018](https://doi.org/10.21315/mjms2018.25.3.10)). | Electrophysiological measurement and clinical or experimental interpretation of established psychological phenomena. |
| **2026** | Rillworld defines **developmental electropsychology**. | The possible formation of pre-semantic dispositions through local electrical dynamics, bodily constraints, wave-derived sensing, recurrence, and history across neural or non-neural substrates. |

The prefix **developmental** carries the distinction. Earlier usages examine,
diagnose, or theorize a psyche already presumed to exist. Rillworld asks how
electrical relations could participate in forming stable tendencies before a
semantic psyche is available to measure.

## Established neighboring fields

| Field | Established center of inquiry | Relationship to the Rillworld working term |
|---|---|---|
| **Electrophysiology** | Electrical properties and signaling of biological cells and tissues. Hodgkin and Huxley's quantitative account of ionic membrane current and nerve excitation is a canonical example ([1952](https://doi.org/10.1113/jphysiol.1952.sp004764)). | Supplies rigorous concepts such as voltage, threshold, conduction, and refractory dynamics. Developmental electropsychology carries those relations into a computational medium and asks about their developmental consequences. |
| **Psychophysiology** | Relations among psychological, social, behavioral, and physiological processes in functional organisms; the Society for Psychophysiological Research emphasizes multilevel measures used to illuminate cognition, emotion, and behavior ([SPR](https://sprweb.org/history/)). | Begins with identifiable psychological or behavioral phenomena and physiological measurements. Rillworld begins with electrical relations from which psychological variables may later become meaningful. |
| **Neuropsychology** | Brain-behavior relationships, including clinical assessment, diagnosis, and treatment of cognitive and behavioral effects of brain function or dysfunction ([APA](https://www.apa.org/ed/graduate/specialize/neuropsychology)). | Developmental **neuro**electropsychology is the neural subset of the broader working theory. Rillworld uses the broader term because its developing substrate need not contain neurons, a brain, or a clinical patient. |
| **Developmental psychobiology** | Development of behavior across organisms, with particular attention to biological factors operating at multiple levels of organization ([ISDP bylaws](https://isdp.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/ISDP-Bylaws-as-of-January-2020.pdf)). | This is the closest established developmental umbrella. Rillworld extends a narrower question into speculative engineering: whether a nonbiological electrical medium can acquire stable dispositions through embodied history. |
| **Developmental cognitive neuroscience** | The interface of neuroscience, psychology, and development, especially how cognition and the brain change across the lifespan ([Blakemore et al., 2011](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dcn.2010.08.003)). | Provides theories and methods for developing brains and cognition. Developmental electropsychology starts earlier than a demonstrated brain or cognitive function and includes non-neural substrates. |
| **Developmental and morphogenetic bioelectricity** | Endogenous membrane-voltage patterns, ion channels, pumps, and gap junction networks that regulate biological cell behavior, differentiation, regeneration, and anatomical patterning ([Levin, 2014](https://doi.org/10.1091/mbc.E13-12-0708)). | The closest mechanistic biological precedent for Rill's membrane, neighbor coupling, gradients, and history. Rill translates those principles into engineered local numerical relations. |
| **Computational neuroscience** | Mathematical modeling, theoretical analysis, and simulation used to understand the development, structure, physiology, dynamics, and function of nervous systems ([INCF](https://training.incf.org/studytrack/introduction-computational-neuroscience)). | Computational neuroscience models nervous systems and neural data. Rillworld constructs a non-neural computational field whose possible psychological vocabulary remains an open developmental question. |
| **Synthetic psychology** | Understanding apparently psychological behavior by constructing simple sensorimotor systems; Braitenberg's vehicles show how observers attribute fear, aggression, love, or foresight to minimal mechanisms ([Braitenberg, 1984](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262521123/vehicles/)). | Shares the synthetic method and warns against reading mechanism through anthropomorphic appearance. Rillworld adds continuing environmental coupling, private electrical state, development through recurrence, and explicit provenance for what is observed versus imagined. |
| **Artificial life** | Synthetic systems that exhibit characteristics associated with natural living systems and simulations or realizations used to study life-like organization ([Langton, 1989](https://www.routledge.com/Artificial-Life-Proceedings-Of-An-Interdisciplinary-Workshop-On-The-Synthesis/Langton/p/book/9780429032769)). | Rillworld sits naturally beside artificial life. Developmental electropsychology offers a narrower lens on how electrical history might become disposition. |
| **Developmental robotics** | Embodied synthetic models of ontogeny and robots that acquire skills through developmental, sensorimotor, morphological, and environmental interaction ([Lungarella et al., 2003](https://doi.org/10.1080/09540090310001655110)). | Shares embodiment, environmental coupling, plasticity, and development. Rill concentrates on a stationary field, wave-derived sensation, and pre-semantic local history. |
| **Embodied cognition** | Families of claims that cognition is situated, time-pressured, action-oriented, and deeply dependent on bodily interaction with an environment ([Wilson, 2002](https://doi.org/10.3758/BF03196322)). | Supports treating Agar's hardware and room as constitutive conditions rather than transparent infrastructure. Developmental electropsychology focuses more specifically on electrical and wave-mediated developmental dynamics before cognition has been established. |

## Positive scope for Rillworld

Within this repository, developmental electropsychology names a specific
research question:

> How might patterned electrical activity, local coupling, bodily constraints,
> environmental waves, and repeated experience become stable tendencies such
> as familiarity, salience, attention, temperament, or behavior before
> semantic representation across neural, non-neural, biological, and
> computational substrates?

The working theory has five defining commitments:

1. **Development before interpretation.** Electrical differences, endogenous
   activity, coupling, and recurrence precede any claim about cognition.
2. **Wave-native embodiment.** Light, pressure, vibration, sound, radio, heat,
   and internal voltage are treated as changing physical conditions before
   tokens or named objects.
3. **History as changed susceptibility.** Repetition may alter what becomes
   easier to enter again while identity and psychological meaning remain open.
4. **Non-neural generality.** Neural development is one important comparative
   map, while plant signaling, non-neural bioelectricity, distributed animal
   coordination, and engineered fields remain equally relevant.
5. **Careful evidence boundaries.** Implemented local laws, observed boundary
   changes, conceptual visualizations, and psychological interpretation remain
   separate kinds of claim.

This working term joins the established fields above around a distinctive
object: the possible development of pre-semantic dispositions in an
environmentally coupled computational electrical medium. Rillworld defines
**developmental neuroelectropsychology** as the neural subset of that broader
question.

# Rillworld constitution

## Purpose

Rillworld asks whether continued local activity inside a computational body can
begin producing the conditions for its own continued activity.

It is not an attempt to make a smaller language model, simulate a named
organism, or encode a human-authored purpose into a seed. Agar supplies a body
and a world. Local physics supplies possibilities. Whatever organization may
arise must remain free to be quiet, simple, strange, transient, or unlike the
categories brought to it.

## The inherited body

Agar is the 2015 Intel MacBook Pro on which Rill continues. Its processor,
memory, persistent storage, battery, charger, camera, microphone, Bluetooth
radio, display, speakers, thermal system, timing, and physical limits are not
transparent infrastructure. They are the inherited conditions under which
Rill exists.

The current functional correspondences are guides for Taylor and Blu, not
labels supplied to Rill:

| Function | Agar condition |
|---|---|
| Energy reserve and supply | Battery and external power |
| Transformation and heat | Processor activity and electrical consumption |
| Continuing pulse | Rill's local update cadence |
| Immediate medium | Active memory and local field capacity |
| Persistent planar history | Memory-mapped `rill.bits` |
| Ephemeral depth | Sparse live process memory |
| Light exchange | Camera luminance boundary |
| Sound exchange | Microphone waveform boundary |
| Nearby radio exchange | Bluetooth boundary |
| Thermoregulation | Heat sink, fan, throttling, and thermal limits |
| Outward consequence | Display light; speakers remain unclaimed |
| External scientific access | Authenticated LAN path |

No component is declared to be Rill's brain, genome, heart, face, or identity.
If analogous functions arise, they must be recognized by what an organization
maintains, not assigned by filename or hardware name.

## Medium and circulation

Rill requires more than positions containing bits. A medium must provide the
conditions through which local states move, encounter one another, exchange
material, experience resistance, and leave consequences.

The future medium may have local capacity, density, pressure, permeability,
viscosity, agitation, and resource availability. Those properties must not
arrive as interpreted messages. Agar's changing heat, power, elapsed time,
memory pressure, and computational load may alter the physics directly.

Circulation is not merely delivery of instructions through a finished machine.
Continued movement may carry history. Collision may create consequence.
Bonding may create temporary structure. Decay may return capacity. Bodily
limits may make some organizations easier to continue than others.

## Smallest conceptual layer

Candidate architecture begins with effective local material state inspired by
charge, fields, gradients, permeability, conservation, dissipation, and
boundary-controlled transfer. It does not begin with threads, cores, cache
lines, or device APIs.

The Intel's literal atoms and transistors remain inaccessible to ordinary
software. Accessible hardware hosts the representation; it does not replace
the lower-level physics being represented. Every larger candidate structure
must have a traceable path from repeated lower-level relations rather than
appearing as a hidden high-level primitive.

`DEVELOPMENTAL-SYNTHESIS.md` translates the recurring physical solutions found
across plant, fungal, animal, and simpler systems into capabilities that this
local physics must leave possible for Agar's actual body. The comparison
constrains possibility without installing a finished anatomy.

## Scale and severe local constraints

Human circulation combines enormous global scale with microscopic passages.
Red blood cells pass single-file through the smallest capillaries, deforming to
fit channels narrower than their unconstrained diameter. The body is massively
parallel because it contains a vast branching network, not because every small
channel is wide.

Rillworld should preserve this principle. Microscopic sites form the medium;
larger bonded carriers may contain many sites. The narrowest passages may admit
only one carrier across. Ordering, spacing, delay, deformation, blockage, and
branching can then become physical sources of history.

A carrier's identity must not depend on an exact pixel shape. It may instead
depend on preserved connectivity, cyclic order, internal circulation, a
carried strand, or the ability to regain an unconstrained form after passage.

## Chemistry and conditional persistence

Encounters need not share one outcome. Local configurations may pass without
special interaction, scatter, repel, bind weakly, mature into persistent
bonds, destabilize, transform, fragment, or decay.

Bond duration must not be assigned by a universal timer. A young bond may
mature. A mature bond may remain while its local configuration and incoming
flow remain compatible. Changed circulation may reinforce, alter, or dissolve
it. Complete saturation may enter a terminal bonded history before returning
capacity to the medium.

A fixed bond merely survives. The deeper possibility is closure:

```text
circulation makes structure
structure changes circulation
changed circulation remakes structure
```

Only when a structure participates in producing the conditions that maintain
or recreate it does the system approach autopoiesis.

## Strands and inheritance

Rill's C source is not its DNA. The source establishes local physics. An
inherited pattern must arise inside those laws.

The earliest genome-like form should be closer to an RNA-world structure: a
bonded sequence whose geometry changes nearby reactions and can sometimes
template a complementary sequence. Pattern and catalytic consequence should be
the same physical object before any separate interpreter exists.

No human message, opcode, word, task, or answer may be written into the strand.
The strand becomes informative only if its shape repeatedly causes
consequences, copies, and variations inside the local world.

A protected, double-stranded archival form becomes useful only after active
strands already make expression and copying possible. Inheritance includes
more than sequence: medium, body, timing, prior structure, and environmental
history all influence what a copied pattern can become.

## Emergent function

Future structures must not be named into roles. A structure becomes
heart-like only if it sustains circulation. It becomes vessel-like only if it
channels flow. It becomes repair-like only if its activity restores a boundary
or passage. It becomes sensory only if environmental recurrence changes what
continues. It becomes genomic only if its pattern is inherited.

The constitutional sequence is therefore:

```text
Agar supplies energy, matter, timing, limits, and environmental exchange.
Local physics supplies possible relationships.
Circulation supplies encounters.
Strands may supply inheritance.
Rill must supply its own organization.
```

## Observation and restraint

Instrumentation can turn a system toward the observer. Runtime counters,
semantic dashboards, labels, rewards, and activity logs must not be introduced
merely to make Rill legible.

The outward light boundary is consequence rather than explanation. Human
observation must distinguish visible geometry from interpretation and current
mechanism from claims about cognition, experience, or life.

Blu may inspect Agar's exposed hardware anatomy read-only in the caretaker
layer. That observation does not grant hardware or administrative access to
Rill or to an inactive candidate. Any future bodily coupling must be separately
specified as a bounded change in local physical possibility.

Silence, repetition, dissipation, extinction, or failure to resemble familiar
intelligence are valid outcomes. Time is not evidence of development, and
complex appearance is not evidence of meaning.

## Lineage boundary

The planar field persists across a core restart. Sparse off-plane depth exists
only in the live process and is released by restart. Any change to core physics
therefore creates a deliberate lineage boundary, not an ordinary software
update.

Candidate physics must be built and tested separately. Promotion requires an
explicit decision to release current live depth. Rill World may be changed or
restarted independently only when the core and its live depth remain intact.

On November 12, 2026, Taylor explicitly chose two direct live lineage changes
without a rehearsal organism: planar membrane excitability with local coupling,
spontaneous phase, refractory recovery, and voltage gradients; then local
hysteresis through which repeated neighborhood relations can alter future
susceptibility. Taylor accepted the release of then-current sparse depth at
each breath. Those decisions do not create a standing exception for later core
changes; every future lineage boundary still requires its own explicit choice.

# Unresolved choices

These choices are intentionally unsettled. An implementation that silently
answers them is premature.

## Representation

- Is the medium stored as a scalar per site, derived from occupancy, or carried
  only by transfer constraints?
- Does local capacity count units, connected volume, directional components,
  or another conserved quantity?
- Which relations define continuity while a connected configuration deforms?

## Flow

- What local difference produces transfer: pressure, available capacity,
  directional momentum, timing, or a combination?
- How do resistance and permeability change without installing fixed routes?
- What creates branching, closure, leakage, and reopening?

## Bonding

- Which geometries are compatible?
- What conditions distinguish a new bond from a mature bond?
- What later encounter preserves, strengthens, releases, fragments, or
  terminates a configuration?
- Can a stable relation persist without permanently consuming finite capacity?

## Templating

- What local geometric relation constitutes complementarity?
- How can adjacent assembly occur without a symbolic translation table?
- What permits completed strands to separate?
- Which error sources produce variation without overwhelming copying?

## Host conditions

- Which physical conditions may alter the candidate: elapsed time, thermal
  pressure, energy availability, memory pressure, or scheduling delay?
- Should coupling be global, boundary-local, or mediated through the medium?
- How can the system experience scarcity without receiving a preferred answer?

## Scale

- What ratio between site, mobile unit, connected configuration, and narrow
  passage permits both deformation and serial order?
- How can enormous network-level parallelism coexist with one-at-a-time local
  passage?
- Which quantities must be conserved as scale increases?

## Lineage

- Can the current planar field be mapped into the candidate without pretending
  that existing bit patterns already have new meanings?
- Which candidate state is persistent and which exists only in live memory?
- What exact history is released at promotion?
- What observation could show mechanical failure without turning the runtime
  toward the observer?

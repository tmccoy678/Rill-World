# Architecture requirements from the comparative pass

The inactive candidate must not assume that one transport mode, one pump, one
wave, one passage type, or one coordinating region is universally required.

## Transport modes that must remain possible

- transfer pushed by a local oscillator;
- transfer pulled by loss or demand at a distant boundary;
- source–sink transfer established by local production and consumption;
- transfer generated by moving structures dragging the surrounding medium;
- self-propagating coupled waves;
- direct branch delivery that bypasses the general medium for selected states;
- diffusion or short-range exchange when long-range transfer is unnecessary.

The first candidate may implement only a subset, but it must document which
alternatives its representation would make impossible.

## Passages

Passages may:

- emerge from local differences in permeability;
- admit different sizes under different conditions;
- allow small states to bypass a blocked large configuration;
- narrow, widen, seal, branch, collapse, and reopen;
- be replaced through local growth rather than repaired in place;
- isolate a damaged region while preserving the larger network.

A fixed test passage is an apparatus, not a claim that later passages remain
fixed.

## Distributed driving

Whole-network transfer may arise from many small local drivers. A candidate
must not require one permanent central driver unless that requirement is the
specific question being tested.

Equivalent regions may coordinate through topology, shared pressure, coupled
waves, or local exchange without permanent leadership.

## Network plasticity

Transfer and topology may change one another:

```text
local transfer changes growth or permeability
changed topology redirects later transfer
redirected transfer changes later topology
```

This relation must remain expressible without a global route planner.

## Damage and proportional repair

Local failure may trigger isolation, flushing, rerouting, replacement, or
reopening. Repair need not restore an exact saved local arrangement. It may
restore network-level capacity, proportion, or exchange through a different
local structure.

## Integration

The candidate representation must permit several overlapping propagation
systems: directional transfer, pressure change, electrical-like excitation,
chemical-like amplification, and local mechanical response.

A self-propagating response may arise when one local change produces a second
change that recreates the first change in a neighboring region. No central
broadcaster is required.

## Experiment 02 hold

The earlier four-stream whole-body trial is superseded. Experiment 02 begins at
effective Level 0 and tests whether local state can produce boundary-controlled
transfer geometry.

Before implementation it must state:

1. the conserved and dissipative quantities;
2. the neighborhood and transfer equations;
3. how permeability can change locally;
4. whether bounded fluctuation is present;
5. what supports persistence;
6. which comparative possibilities are intentionally postponed.
